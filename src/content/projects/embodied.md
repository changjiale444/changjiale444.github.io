## Overview

Affiliation: [Xiamen University MAC Lab](https://mac.xmu.edu.cn/).
Developing an embodied simulation platform for scientific laboratory environments using Blender, Habitat-Sim, and LabUtopia assets, with support for RGB-D perception, navigable meshes, multi-floor navigation, and workflow-constrained tasks.

## System architecture
The current platform connects **laboratory assets → scene construction → navigation meshes → RGB-D observations → workflow-constrained navigation**. This is a conceptual system description; a verified architecture figure will be added when available.

## Research methods
The research plan explores a structured scientific program, graph-based consistency checking, navigation utility constraints, 3D laboratory generation, and navigation episode generation. These are ongoing research directions rather than completed experimental claims.

## Current results
The supplied CV reports 32 three-floor scenes and 768 workflow navigation tasks, with 32/32 scenes passing reachability validation and an 87.5% workflow-navigation success rate for Workflow Graph. These are author-reported preliminary results; evaluation protocol, baselines, and experiment artifacts are pending documentation.
